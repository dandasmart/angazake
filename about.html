About Angazake

Built for every citizen
of Masinga.

Angazake is Kenya's first sub-county accountability platform — putting verified, real-time public project data in the hands of every citizen, from Kivaa to Muthesya.

🌐 Website 📲 Mobile App 💬 WhatsApp Bot 📞 USSD *XXX# ✅ Verified Data 🆓 Free Forever
Our Mission

Transparency. Accountability. Action.

Every year, billions of shillings are allocated to Masinga Sub-County through CDF, Ward Development Funds, County and National Government budgets. Yet most citizens have no way to know what was built, what stalled, or where the money went.

Angazake changes that. We track every project, verify every claim, and publish everything — so that every citizen, every journalist, and every leader knows exactly what is happening in their ward.

"Wazi. Sasa. Kwa Wote." — Open. Now. For All.

Data Integrity
How we verify every record

We don't just publish what we're told. Every project goes through a rigorous verification process before it goes live.

📝
1

Citizen Submission

Any citizen submits a project report with location, description and photo via app, website or WhatsApp.

🔍
2

Initial Review

Our team reviews the submission for completeness within 48 hours and cross-checks against official records.

📍
3

Site Verification

A trained Wazi Verification Agent visits the physical site, takes GPS-tagged photos and confirms all details.

✅
4

Published Live

Verified records go live on all four channels — website, app, WhatsApp and USSD — within 10 days.

Our Values
What we stand for
🔍

Radical Transparency

Every record, every source, every verification step is open and auditable. We never publish unverified data as fact.

⚖️

Political Independence

Angazake is not affiliated with any political party or candidate. We track all leaders equally — CDF, MCA, Governor, MP and Senator.

🤝

Citizen First

The platform is free for all citizens. We will never charge a citizen to access public information about public funds.

🌍

Inclusivity

We actively track marginalised wards and flag areas receiving fewer projects — so no community is left behind silently.

🔒

Data Privacy

Citizen reporter identities are kept private. We comply with Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019 and ODPC guidelines.

📈

Continuous Improvement

We update records quarterly, welcome corrections from officials and citizens, and publish our own performance metrics.

⚖️ Legal Framework

Angazake operates under Kenya's Access to Information Act 2016, Data Protection Act 2019, and the Constitution of Kenya 2010 (Article 35 — Right to Information).

🔒 Data Policy

Citizen reporter details are never published. All project data is sourced from public records or verified field visits. Right of reply is available to named contractors and officials.

📍 Pilot Status

Angazake is currently in pilot phase covering Masinga Sub-County, Machakos County. Full county rollout is planned for 2027 subject to funding and verification capacity.
